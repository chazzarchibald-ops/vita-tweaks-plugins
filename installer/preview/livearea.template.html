The Vita's own parts are real. The status bar, the three buttons, the gate's frame, the Start button and the page curl are cut from a screenshot of your Vita, so every design sits exactly where the console puts it. The gate covers the middle of the background, so each design keeps its name clear of it.

While the page opens or closes, the background shows for a moment with nothing over it, so each design has to look finished that way too.

The Vita takes these pictures in 256 colours. Soft glows pick up a fine grain there, as the current one has; flat designs such as Blueprint and Slash lose the least.
